# Task 5 – Strategic Recommendations

## Overview

This task focuses on developing data-driven strategic recommendations for a hypothetical fashion brand based on the findings from the previous stages of Fashion Market Research and Predictive Modelling.

The objective is to convert the insights obtained from data analysis and predictive modelling into practical recommendations related to product development, pricing strategy, target demographics, inventory management, and sustainability.

## Key Areas Covered

The recommendations developed in this task focus on:

* Product Development
* Pricing Strategy
* Target Demographics
* Inventory Management
* Sustainability Strategy

## Data-Driven Basis

The recommendations are supported by findings from the fashion market dataset and the predictive modelling performed in the previous task.

A Random Forest Regression model was used to predict `Price_INR`. The model achieved:

* **R² Score:** 0.7425
* **MAE:** ₹26,564.31
* **RMSE:** ₹56,975.68

The feature importance analysis showed that:

* **Stock_Units:** 0.642773
* **Ratings:** 0.154105
* **Sub_Category_Handbags:** 0.041852

Other variables such as Sustainability Score, Target Age Group, Segment, product category, and other product characteristics were also considered during the analysis.

These findings were used as supporting evidence for developing the strategic recommendations.

## Anticipated Outcomes

The proposed recommendations are expected to help a hypothetical fashion brand:

* Make more informed product development decisions
* Support structured and data-driven pricing decisions
* Improve customer targeting
* Manage inventory more effectively
* Incorporate sustainability into product planning
* Use analytical insights to support future business decisions

## Deliverable

The final deliverable for this task is a report containing the strategic recommendations, their data-driven rationale, and anticipated outcomes.
